The basics Everyone

Signing in

  • Accounts are made by an administrator or shipping manager. There is no public sign-up.
  • Your first password is temporary: you'll be asked to choose your own before you can do anything else.
  • To change your password later, open your name in the top right, choose My Account, then Change Password. My Account is also where you update your name, email and phone number.
  • For security you're signed out after 72 hours without using the app, and at most 14 days after signing in. Just sign in again.

How a shipment fits together

Supplies travel in layers. Items can go into any of the layers directly, so a loose item can sit in a container without a box.

  • Shipment sent from one warehouse to another
    • Container a shipping container
      • Pallet
        • Box
          • Items
      • Box
        • Items
      • Items

Where something sits is set on its Edit form (a box's pallet or container, a pallet's container, a container's shipment). It's fine to leave this blank until you know. Each record's page shows its full location at the top, like Home / SHIPMENT-57 / CONTAINER-57 / PALLET-402.

Statuses

StatusMeans
WarehousedSitting in a warehouse, not yet planned for a shipment. Boxes, pallets and loose items only.
StagedReady and waiting to be put into a shipment. Boxes, pallets and loose items only.
In ProgressBeing packed. Volunteers can still change it.
CompletePacked and closed, ready to ship or on its way.
ReceivedArrived. The receiving warehouse can now unpack it.
ArchivedFinished with and kept for the record.

Statuses flow downwards: marking a shipment or container Complete, Received or Archived does the same to everything inside it, and a pallet's status passes to its boxes. Making a box or pallet Staged or Warehoused takes it out of whatever it was in.

Finding things

  • Jump To (top of most pages) opens a box or pallet by its Custom ID, searches items, and links to the newest box, pallet, container and shipment you can open.
  • The item, box, pallet, container and shipment lists have a Filters panel above the table: status, created dates, who created it, and where it's headed (its receiving warehouse, or None if it isn't in a shipment yet). Items can also be filtered by category and by whether they're used anywhere. A filtered list has its own link, so you can bookmark or share it.
  • Dropdowns with long lists have a search box: start typing to narrow them down, use the arrow keys, and press Enter to choose.

The dashboard

The home page shows activity for the time range you pick (30 days, Year or All time): what's been packed, a leaderboard, your own totals and milestones, and fun facts. Managers also see shipments, data quality and where supplies were unpacked. Click a card's header to collapse it; the app remembers which cards you've closed.

Deleting

  • Deleting asks you to confirm first.
  • For items, shipments, containers, and boxes or pallets that have been received, you also type delete, and the confirmation lists what else is affected (for example, which boxes would be unassigned).
  • Things still in use can't be deleted: a hospital that has received supplies, a category or warehouse that's in use, or a person who has created records. Deactivate them instead.

Packing Volunteers Shipping managers

Volunteers catalogue items and pack them into boxes, pallets and containers. You can create boxes and pallets, and change anything that is still In Progress; once something is Complete you can still see it but not change it.

Creating items

Every item gets a name built from its parts, so the same thing always ends up with the same name and isn't entered twice. Fill in only the fields that apply:

  • Base Name (required): the plain thing itself, without sizes or brands. Gloves, not Medium nitrile gloves 50 pack.
  • Brand/Model: the maker and model, e.g. Stryker FL28C.
  • S/N: the serial number, if it has one. It's added to the end of the name, so two otherwise identical beds stay separate.
  • Unit Weight (kg): the weight of one. When you pack the item, its weight is filled in for you from this.
  • Size, measurements, area and range: under Additional Measurement Fields, for things like 25ga, 15mL, 5x10cm or 1-5mm.
  • Category, Notes, and Flag for Review if you're not sure you've got it right.

For example, a U-Line brand, Nitrile Gloves base name, Medium size and a 50-Pack measurement always become U-Line Nitrile Gloves Medium 50-Pack. Search before creating: the item may already exist.

Packing items

Open a box, pallet or container and go to its items page. There are three tabs:

  • Search: find an existing item and add it. Before you search, Your recent items lists the last things you packed, ready to add again.
  • New: create a new item and pack it in one go.
  • Manage: change the quantity, weight or expiry date of what's already in it, or remove something.

Tap an item to open its form: quantity, weight (filled in from the item's unit weight until you type your own), expiry date, and Generate ID? if the item needs its own ID number on its label.

Packing with the keyboard

On a computer you can pack without the mouse:

  1. The search box is ready when the page opens. Type the item's name and press Enter.
  2. Press Enter again to open the first result (or use ↓ ↑ to pick another and press Enter).
  3. Type the quantity (it's already selected) and press Enter to add it.
  4. The page comes back with the cursor in the search box for the next item. Esc clears the search.

Staging and Warehouse areas

Loose items, boxes and pallets that aren't in anything yet live in the Staging Area (ready to ship) or the Warehouse Area (in storage). Both menus list them and let you add items straight there.

Moving things (Mass Action)

Tick the rows you want to move in a list, then press Mass Action and choose where they go: a box, pallet or container, the Staging or Warehouse area, or their current location. You can only move things into places you're allowed to pack into, and if anything can't be moved, nothing is.

Duplicating boxes

When several boxes are packed the same way, open one and press Duplicate to make up to 10 copies, with or without its pallet or container assignment.

Managing shipments and items Shipping managers Admins

Shipping managers can do everything volunteers can, on any box, pallet or container, plus the following.

Reconciliation

The Reconciliation menu keeps the item catalogue tidy. Each list shows how far through it you are:

  • Unverified: new items to check. Opening one searches for likely duplicates; tick them to merge them into this item (everything packed under the duplicates moves across), and optionally delete the empty duplicates. Verify the item when it's right.
  • Uncategorized: items without a category.
  • User-flagged: items someone flagged for review.

Categories

Categories can be renamed at any time and every item in them follows. A category can't be deleted while items or pallets use it.

Getting a shipment ready

  1. Check every box is on a pallet or in a container, every pallet is in a container, and every container is in the shipment. The shipment page lists anything still staged or warehoused that could be added.
  2. Make sure the shipment has the right shipping and receiving warehouses: the receiving warehouse is who will be able to unpack it.
  3. Mark containers Complete as they're closed. When the shipment leaves, it's marked Complete, and everything inside follows.

Accounts and passwords

  • Create accounts from Admin › Users. You set a temporary password and the person chooses their own when they first sign in.
  • If someone forgets their password, open their profile and set a new temporary one.
  • To stop someone signing in, set their status to Deactivated. They're signed out straight away.
  • Shipping managers can't change or delete Admin accounts, or make anyone an Admin.

Unpacking Receiving managers

Receiving managers work in one warehouse. You see shipments once they've been marked Received into your warehouse, and you record where every item goes: each hand-over to a hospital, facility or clinic is an unpacking.

Your warehouse's stock

  • Your dashboard shows units in stock, stock by category, items expiring in the next 90 days, stock untouched for 60+ days, and where things have been unpacked to, including destinations that haven't received anything in 90+ days.
  • The Unpack Items menu has List All (everything with stock left), Browse by Category, and Unpacking History.
  • Each item shows where it is, nested as shipment › container › pallet › box.

Unpacking items

  1. Find the item, in List All, by category, or by searching, or open a received box or pallet and go to its Unpack tab.
  2. Enter how many are going out (it can't be more than what's left), choose the hospital, facility or clinic, and the date.
  3. Add a note if useful, and save.

The next unpack form starts on the same hospital you just used, and on the same date if you picked it today, so unpacking a whole delivery for one clinic is quick.

Unpacking lots at once

  • Unpack All on a received box or pallet sends everything left in it to one destination.
  • From an item's locations page you can tick several and unpack them together.
  • If something can't be unpacked (for example, it's already all gone), the message says which ones and why; the rest still go through.

Hospitals, facilities and clinics

You manage the destinations for your warehouse. If one is missing while you're unpacking, use the Don't see your hospital/facility/clinic? Add one. link under the destination list. A destination that has received supplies can't be deleted; deactivate it instead.

Fixing mistakes

  • To correct the date of an unpacking, open the box, pallet or container it came from, go to its History tab, change the date and press Update.
  • To undo an unpacking completely, delete it from the item's History tab. The quantity goes back into stock.

Running the app Admins

Admins can do everything, in every warehouse.

Receiving a shipment

When a shipment arrives, mark it Received. Everything inside becomes Received and appears for the receiving warehouse's managers to unpack. Until then they can't see it.

Warehouses

Warehouses are where stock is, and who works there. Give every person a warehouse on their profile (receiving managers only see their own), and give every shipment a shipping and a receiving warehouse.

Exports

Spreadsheet downloads (CSV files, which open in Excel or Google Sheets):

  • Shipment: everything in it, grouped by item or by category, with quantities, weights and locations. Use Export on the shipment's page.
  • Hospital: what one destination received between two dates. Use Export on its page.
  • Unpacking history: every unpacking between two dates, for one destination or all. Use Export on the Unpacking History page.
