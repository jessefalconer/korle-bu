Developer Updates

19 Nov 2026

A fresh coat of paint and a faster app:

  • Easier on your phone. Pop-up windows now slide up from the bottom of the screen and use the full width, with bigger buttons that are easier to tap.

  • Cleaner look. Updated buttons, menus and text throughout, with everything lined up neatly.

  • Faster pages. Lists, boxes and pallets load more quickly, especially when they hold a lot of items.

  • Better item search when reconciling. Results come back faster and match what you type more reliably.

  • Security and upgrades. The software that runs the app has been upgraded to newer, supported versions with the latest security fixes. Unused pages and features were removed, so there is less to keep secure.

  • Small fixes. The KBNF logo shows again on the help pages, and tapping an option's label now selects it.

Found something that doesn't look right? Use About → Report a Bug in the menu on your phone.

 

Jun 2025

  • More reliable statuses. Boxes and pallets now update their status correctly when they are moved, staged or warehoused.

 

Mar 2025

  • Unpacking dates. Each unpacking record now has a date, and you can change it right from the list.

 

Jan 2025

  • Better duplicates. Duplicating a box now copies item weights too.

 

Aug 2024

  • Sign-in protection. Repeated failed sign-in attempts are now slowed down, which makes it much harder for someone to guess a password.

  • Distribution exports. Unpacking history can be exported to a spreadsheet, filtered by date and hospital.

  • New look and speed. A styling refresh, clearer page numbers and faster loading pages.

 

2023

  • Unpack faster. Unpack all items on a pallet at once, or pick several items while browsing by category.

  • Duplicate boxes. Make copies of a box and its contents in one step.

  • Hospital exports. Each hospital, facility or clinic page lists what it has received, and can export it.

 

2022

  • Unpack a whole box. Send every item in a box to a hospital in one step.

  • Archiving. Admins can archive old records so they stay out of everyone's way.

  • Receiving managers can now add and manage their own hospitals and clinics.

 

2021

  • Mass actions. Select several items, boxes or pallets and move them all at once.

  • Jump To. Go straight to a box or pallet by its ID, or search for an item, from any page.

  • Unpacking history. See a full history of what was unpacked, where and when.

  • Leaderboards on the dashboard, just for fun.
