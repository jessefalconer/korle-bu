About this page

KBNF Inventory is an internal tool for the Korle Bu Neuroscience Foundation's staff and volunteers. Accounts are set up by an administrator; there is no public sign-up. This page explains what the app keeps about you, and why.

What we keep

  • Your account: name, email address, phone number, your role and warehouse, and your password (stored only in scrambled form, so no one can read it).
  • Optional extras you or an administrator add: personal notes and the dashboard's achievement details.
  • Your work in the app: the items, boxes, pallets and shipments you create, pack or unpack are recorded with your name and the date.
  • Technical records: the server keeps logs that include your IP address, to keep the app running and secure.

Why

To run the inventory: to know what was shipped where and by whom, to show activity and the dashboard leaderboard, and so administrators can contact you about your account. We don't use your information for anything else.

Who can see it

  • Other people signed in to the app can see your name on the records you made and on the leaderboard.
  • Administrators and shipping managers can see account details, including email addresses and phone numbers.
  • We don't sell or share your information, and the app has no advertising or tracking.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. It ends after 72 hours without activity, or 14 days after you sign in, whichever comes first. There are no other cookies.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. Because inventory records are kept as a history of what was shipped, your name stays on the records you made even if your account is later deactivated.

Questions, corrections or removal

You can change most of your own details under My Account. For anything else, including a copy of what we hold or asking for something to be corrected or removed, email jesse@kbnf.org.
